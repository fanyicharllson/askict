## Where do I see the timetable and the calendar on the ERP?

The menu contains Faculty Calendar, Course Timetable and Exam Timetable. They are read-only pages. Timetable changes, deadlines and events are also posted on the Noticeboard and delivered as notifications under the bell. The semester switcher (calendar icon) shows another academic year or semester.
