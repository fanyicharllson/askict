## Can I transfer from another university?

Yes. For all transfer cases the academic transcripts must be authenticated by the issuing institution. The student's file is sent to the Deans of the respective faculties, who evaluate the documents to decide which credits can be transferred based on previous academic achievements. When the credits are validated, approval is sent to the Admissions Office (admissions@ictuniversity.edu.cm), which admits the student into the program. Top-Up students (HND holders) also provide a certified copy of the HND diploma, and their files are checked by the Department of Transcript and Verification.
