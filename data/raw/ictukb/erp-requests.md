## Which requests can I submit on the ERP?

Under Requests and Services choose the request type and the turnaround (standard or express, where offered), state the purpose, and submit. The fee, if any, is added to My Fees. The Registrar's Office processes each request, and you can follow its status and reference number on the page. Available requests include: visa-support letter, attestation or enrolment letter, certificate verification; partial or full official transcript; name correction, leave of absence, deferment, withdrawal or reinstatement (give the reason and the date requested).

## What other student services are in the ERP?

My ID Card (apply for the student ID card, print it once issued, or apply for a replacement; the QR code on the back lets staff check the card); My Admission Letter (view and print at any time); Change of Major; Evaluate Lecturers (anonymous, while the evaluation window is open); Internship; Senior Project; Online Studies / Exams (request permission to study or sit exams online); My Clearance.
