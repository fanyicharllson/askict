## What is the ICTU ERP and what can students do with it?

The ICTU ERP is the university's single online platform for academic, financial and administrative work. It runs in any modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone, and can also be installed on a phone like an app. The student address is https://apps.ictuniversity.edu.cm/erp/. The interface can be switched between English and French.

Students use it for: the dashboard (programme, CGPA, fee status, marks, attendance, notifications); course registration; results and result slips; attendance; fees, receipts and the wallet; complaints about marks; resit registration; transcripts; requests and services (letters, official transcript, deferment, withdrawal); the student ID card and admission letter; change of major; lecturer evaluation; internship and senior project; clearance; the faculty calendar, course timetable and exam timetable; the LMS (course materials, live sessions, assignments, quizzes); the library (loans and fines); the university bus; reporting a maintenance problem; and Campus Connect messaging.

## What can I do from the login page?

Forgot password; Apply for admission (public online application form); Check my application (follow an application with its reference number); Verify a record (a free check, with matricule and last name, that a person is a current student or graduate, showing only name, programme and status); Request a certificate verification (a formal paid check for employers and institutions); Student registration (for students who already have a matricule but no ERP account; it stays pending until the Admission Office approves it); Lecturer registration (approved by the faculty Admin Assistant); and Get the app (Windows, Android, or Add to Home Screen on iPhone).

## What are notifications, the noticeboard and the semester switcher?

The bell shows unread notifications (results approved, requests decided, documents ready, fees due) and important events are also emailed. The Noticeboard holds official notices such as timetable changes, deadlines and events. The calendar icon switches the view to another academic year or semester; it only changes your own screen.
