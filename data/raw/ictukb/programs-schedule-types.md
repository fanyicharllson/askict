## Are there evening or weekend classes, and how are programs delivered?

The ICT Master's programs are delivered online or onsite and in English or French. The delivery mode of Bachelor programs is not stated on the website. The undergraduate timetable of Fall 2026 schedules lectures from Monday to Saturday (weekday sessions 7:30-10:30, 10:45-13:45 and 14:00-17:00; Saturday 8:00-11:00 and 11:15-14:15). Evening and weekend-only programs are not described on the website.
