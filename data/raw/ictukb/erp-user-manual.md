## Where is the ERP user manual?

Click your name at the top right and choose User Manual. The manual written for your role opens in the browser and can be downloaded or printed. The student manual covers: Getting Started (login, navigation, notifications, noticeboard, profile, password, language), the Student Portal (dashboard, course registration, results, attendance, online exams, complaints, resits, transcript, fees, requests, ID card, change of major, evaluations, internship and senior project, clearance), the Learning Management System, Campus Connect messaging, the results approval workflow, the grading system reference and frequently asked questions.
