## What is ICT University?

The ICT University (ICT-U) describes itself on its website as "Impacting ICTs in all Academic Disciplines". It operates in Yaounde, Cameroon, with an administrative office in the United States (The ICT University Foundation, Baton Rouge, Louisiana). It has two faculties: the Faculty of Information and Communication Technology and the Faculty of Business Management and Sustainability, and offers Bachelor's, Master's and International MBA programs.

## Which accreditations and recognitions does it hold?

The university is a member of the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs (ACBSP), USA. Its letterhead states a ministry authorization (accreditation): US Charter No. 40023338D, approval No. 02/04505/N/MINESUP/DDES/ESUP/SAC/ebm of 21 September 2012 and 14/0406/MINESUP/SG/DDES DU of 4 July 2014. According to its website, the university is accredited to run some Bachelor's, Master's and IMBA programs under the general framework agreement signed between the University of Buea and ICT-U on 14 March 2023.

## Who are its partners?

Partners shown on official letterheads: Lavras University (Brazil), University of Buea, University of Dschang and MINADER.

## Who leads the university?

According to official letterheads of November 2026: Prof. Victor Mbarika, Founder and President of the Board of Trustees; Prof. Emmanuel Tonye, Vice-Chancellor; Prof. Foluso Ayeni and Prof. Awono Onana Charles, Vice-Presidents of the ICT Foundation; Prof. Jean-Emmanuel Pondi, Honorary Vice-Chancellor; Prof. Alain Vilard Ndi Isoh, Deputy Vice-Chancellor in charge of Internal Control and Evaluation. The Secretary General of ICT University Worldwide is Enow Aaron Agbor (bank account notice of 10 October 2024).
