## Which semester and programs does the timetable cover?

It is the updated Fall 2026 combined ICT and BMS undergraduate lecture timetable, English section, for the first semester of 2026/2027 (the semester starts on Monday 19 October 2026). It lists lectures for Years 1 to 4 of the Faculty of ICT programs (ICT, Software Engineering SEN, Computer Science CS, Information Systems and Networking ISN, Cyber Security CYS, Artificial Intelligence AI, Renewable Energy), the BMS programs (including Accounting and Banking and Finance) and Journalism and Mass Communication. Each line gives the course code and title, the instructor, the hall, and the students or programme concerned.

## What are the lecture hours?

Lectures run from Monday to Saturday. Weekday sessions are 7:30 to 10:30, 10:45 to 13:45 and 14:00 to 17:00. Saturday sessions are 8:00 to 11:00 and 11:15 to 14:15.

## Which halls are used?

J-E. Pondi Hall, George Mbarika Hall, IT Hall, Eric Mbarika Hall, B.S. Chumbow Hall, Terry and Linda Byrd Hall, the Computer Lab, and the Radio and TV Studio.
