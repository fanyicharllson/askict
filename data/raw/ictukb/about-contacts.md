## What is the official postal address of the university?

ICT-U (Yaounde campus): Dispensaire Messassi Avenue, P.O. Box 526, Yaounde, Cameroon. The ICT University Foundation (USA): P.O. Box 86236, Baton Rouge, LA 70879, USA. Both addresses are printed on the letterhead of official university notices and policies of November 2026.

## What are the general phone number and email address?

The website header shows the telephone +237 678 76 40 37 and the email admin@ictuniversity.edu.cm. The ERP footer shows the Faculty of Information and Communication Technology, ICT University Yaounde, telephone +237 682 255 000.

## Which other official email addresses exist?

The letterhead of the Office of the Vice-Chancellor shows admin@ictuniversity.edu.cm and vc@ictuniversity.edu.cm. The letterhead of the Deputy Vice-Chancellor in charge of Internal Control and Evaluation shows dvc@ictuniversity.edu.cm.

## How do I contact the admissions office?

The Admissions Office email is admissions@ictuniversity.edu.cm. Transfer and Top-Up documents are checked by the Department of Transcript and Verification, transcript.verification@ictuniversity.edu.cm (both shown on the website entry requirements pages). Applications are made on the online student portal.
