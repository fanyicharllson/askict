## Can I add or drop a course after registering?

Adding: courses of your programme are registered with Register Selected; a course outside your programme needs a Request approved by the Coordinator; once attendance has started the button is Register (Late) and asks for a reason.

Dropping: use Drop next to a registered course, if the button is offered. Courses that already have marks cannot be dropped. An additional course and late registration are among the fee types in My Fees.
