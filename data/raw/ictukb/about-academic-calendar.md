## When does course registration open and close?

For the first semester 2026/2027, course registration for freshmen runs from Tuesday 20 October to Friday 6 November 2026, and for returning students from Monday 19 October to Friday 6 November 2026.

## When are the examinations?

The first semester examinations run from Monday 25 January to Wednesday 3 February 2027, after the end of lectures and revision (18 to 23 January 2027).

The teaching schedule by week, with public holidays, is on the university website (document web-academic-calendar-2026-2027).

## Have the registration dates changed?

Yes. The most recent notice of the Office of the Bursar (Finance Department) states that course registration closes on Monday 23 November 2026, with a penalty of 10,000 FCFA for registration after that date, and that the second installment of fees is payable from 20 to 30 November 2026. This replaces the 6 November end date in the communique of 25 October 2026.
