## What is the official attendance requirement?

Academic Compliance and Administrative Policy (reference ICTU-VC-POL-2026/01, dated 11 November 2026, issued by the Vice-Chancellor, approved by the University Senate and Management Board, effective on publication, applying to all on-campus and online students): students must keep a minimum cumulative attendance of 80% of all scheduled instructional hours in each registered course. A student below 80% is automatically disqualified from the end-of-semester examinations in the affected course or courses. A student facing debarment receives formal notice and may submit an attendance review request, with medical or official supporting documents, to the Head of Department within the published deadlines before the examinations start. Lecturers keep detailed attendance records and communicate periodic totals to students.

The ERP manual states a default minimum attendance setting of 75%; the 80% of the policy of 11 November 2026 is the official rule.

## What is required to enter a lecture room?

Admission to lectures, laboratories and tutorials is conditional. Students must have settled all tuition and required fee installments according to the official University Fee Payment Schedule of the Finance Office, and must carry a complete official Course Registration Form bearing original signatures and official stamps of both the Finance Office and the Head of Department or Dean of Faculty.

## What is required to sit end-of-semester examinations?

Complete institutional and financial clearance: the Finance Office does not issue examination passes, and students are not admitted to examination halls, without 100% payment of their school fees for the academic period. Ad-hoc payment arrangements or last-minute clearances at the examination doors are prohibited.

## What are the rules for online students and the Moodle platform?

Continuous assessments, quizzes, term papers, assignments and online end-of-semester examinations of online students are administered and submitted exclusively through the official ICT University Moodle platform. Lecturers must not accept assignments or exam papers sent by email, except during a documented system-wide outage announced by an official technical notice of the IT Office. Online students who default on the fee schedule have their Moodle accounts automatically suspended. Sharing login credentials or using a classmate's account to bypass a suspension leads to disciplinary action, including formal academic suspension and forfeiture of course credits, for both the student and the account holder.

## Who is responsible for enforcement?

Lecturers keep attendance logs, check signed and stamped registration forms at the classroom door and refuse email submissions. Heads of Department and Deans verify debarment lists and evaluate attendance appeals. The Finance Office enforces the payment schedule, issues examination passes and stamped registration forms and clears candidates at 100% payment. The IT Office suspends Moodle access for defaulters and audits credential sharing. The Registrar and Academic Affairs keep registration and eligibility records and publish policy directives.

## What is the grading scale?

Grades come from the total of CA (out of 30) and exam (out of 70): A 80-100 (4.0 points), B+ 70-79 (3.5), B 60-69 (3.0), C+ 55-59 (2.5), C 50-54 (2.0, minimum pass), D+ 45-49 (1.5), D 40-44 (1.0), F 0-39 (0.0); totals below 50 are resit eligible and only C and above earn credits. CGPA is the sum of grade points times credits divided by the sum of credits. A resit total is capped at 79. These rules come from the ERP manual.
