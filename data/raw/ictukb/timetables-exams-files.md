## When are the exams?

For the first semester 2026/2027 the examinations are scheduled from Monday 25 January to Wednesday 3 February 2027, after the end of lectures and revision (18 to 23 January 2027), according to the official communique of 25 October 2026. Continuous Assessment 1 is from 14 to 19 December 2026 and Continuous Assessment 2 from 4 to 10 January 2027.

## Where will the exam timetable be published?

The exam timetable is published as a PDF in the university announcements during the examination period. It is not available yet, and the ERP exam timetable page shows no sittings. Until it is published, ask your department or the Admissions office. Resit dates are announced in the same way.
