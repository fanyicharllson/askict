## What is clearance in the ERP and when does it apply?

When you finish your studies or leave the university, the Registrar opens your clearance. My Clearance in the ERP shows each step (library, finance, faculty and others), its status, what you need to do and any note from the office. You are graduation-ready once every required step is cleared. The form and the processing time are not described in the ERP manual.
