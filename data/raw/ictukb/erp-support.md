## Who do I contact if the ERP is not working or my account has a problem?

For a forgotten password where you no longer have access to your email, contact the Database Administrator or your office. The ERP footer shows the Faculty of Information and Communication Technology, ICT University Yaounde, telephone +237 682 255 000. Accounts that are pending are approved by the Admission Office (students) or the faculty Admin Assistant (lecturers).

## Common problems and answers

- I cannot see my results: they are not yet approved by the Dean or Registrar, or you have outstanding fees.
- I cannot register for courses: at least 50% of the semester's tuition must be paid; upload the bank receipt in My Fees so Finance can verify it.
- I uploaded a receipt but my fees did not change: receipts change your balance only once Finance approves them.
- My mark is wrong: raise a complaint for that course and mark type.
- I see an old semester's data: check the semester switcher (calendar icon) and choose the current semester.
- I do not receive emails from the ERP: check the spam folder and the email address in My Profile; notifications always appear under the bell.
