## What happens if I pay late or do not pay?

- Campus and student portal access require payment of the first installment, 50% of the total tuition (fees control resumed on 9 November 2026).
- Course registration requires at least 50% of the semester's tuition to be paid and verified (ERP). A penalty of 10,000 FCFA applies to course registration after the deadline of Monday 23 November 2026.
- Results are hidden in the ERP until fees are paid, and transcripts are refused while fees are outstanding. A moratorium granted by Finance never unlocks results.
- Under the Vice-Chancellor policy of 11 November 2026, students may not enter lecture rooms unless the installments due under the official Fee Payment Schedule are settled, and exam passes are issued only after 100% payment of the fees for the period; last-minute clearance at the examination doors is prohibited.
- Online students who default have their Moodle accounts suspended automatically.
