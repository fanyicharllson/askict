## Which documents do I need for a Bachelor's application (new students)?

To complete an application for a Bachelor's degree in either the Faculty of ICT or the Faculty of Business Management and Sustainability (BMS), provide: a certified copy of the birth certificate; certified copies of the GCE "O" Level certificate, GCE "A" Level certificate, Probatoire and Baccalaureate; and authenticated copies of the GCE "O" Level certificate, GCE "A" Level certificate, Probatoire and Baccalaureate.

## What do Top-Up students (HND holders) need?

The same documents as new students plus a certified copy of the HND diploma from the previous school. The documents are forwarded to the Department of Transcript and Verification (transcript.verification@ictuniversity.edu.cm) for checking.
