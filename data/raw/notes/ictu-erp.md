# What is the ICTU ERP?

ERP stands for Enterprise Resource Planning. The ICT University ERP is the university's new online platform where students manage their academic records and applications. Students log in at https://apps.ictuniversity.edu.cm/erp/student/dashboard.php with their Matricule (student registration number) and their password.

New accounts start with a default password given by the faculty. Change it immediately after your first login for account security. If you do not know your default password or cannot log in, contact the ICT Faculty or the admin office (admin@ictuniversity.edu.cm). Never share your password with anyone, including chatbots.

# Internship and senior project supervision (final year students, 2026)

The ICT Faculty informed all final year students that they must:

1. Log into their student account on the ICT University ERP using their Matricule.
2. Update their password immediately.
3. Apply for internship placement at https://apps.ictuniversity.edu.cm/erp/student/internship.php
4. Apply for senior project supervision at https://apps.ictuniversity.edu.cm/erp/student/senior_project.php

Deadline: Friday, 20th November 2026.

Students who miss the deadline may have problems with the processing and follow-up of their internship and senior project. The notice was marked urgent by the ICT Faculty.
