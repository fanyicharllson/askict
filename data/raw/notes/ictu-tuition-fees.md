# ICT-U Tuition Fee Payment Plan (Office of the Bursar, Finance Department)

All students must make an ICT-U Acceptable Payment Arrangement by the billing due date, or their class attendance can be stopped at any time.

## Payment rules
- Application fee: every applicant pays a non-refundable application fee of 25,000 FCFA (international students: $50).
- After admission, students must pay at least 50% of their semester fee at the bank before they can attend any class.
- Students must upload a copy of the bank receipt on the student portal after each payment.
- Students can only drop out of a semester within 2 weeks from the resumption of classes.
- Additional courses: students enrolled for less than full time still pay full tuition. Students who take extra courses in a semester pay for the additional courses.
- Payment method: the ONLY acceptable method for ALL tuition and other fees (including the student union fee) is payment into ICT-U's bank accounts. Paying a person, staff or faculty member is UNACCEPTABLE, and the student remains responsible for the unpaid fees. All payments must be made in the name of the student and only into the designated bank accounts.

## How to upload a payment receipt on the student portal
Sign in to your student portal → select My Wallet → select Offline → Choose file → fill in the amount on the receipt → add the bank and payment ID information → click Save.

## How to pay the semester fee in the student portal
Sign in to the student portal → select Fee Receipt → click the little plus button → choose the concerned semester → select what item you wish to pay for → click Proceed to Payment.

## Tuition payment deadlines (installments of the semester fee)

The semester fee is paid in three installments: 50%, then 30%, then 20%.

| Installment | Bachelor | Masters | PhD | Intl Bachelor | Intl Masters | Intl PhD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st: 50% | 182,500 F | 237,500 F | 412,500 F | $500 | $1,300 | $1,300 |
| 2nd: 30% | 109,500 F | 142,500 F | 247,500 F | $300 | $780 | $780 |
| 3rd: 20% | 73,000 F | 95,000 F | 165,000 F | $200 | $520 | $520 |
| Total per semester | 365,000 FCFA | 475,000 FCFA | 825,000 FCFA | $1,000 | $2,600 | $2,600 |

Installment dates per semester:
- Fall semester (last installment 30th December): 1st installment from the date of admission to 2nd October; 2nd installment 20th–30th November; 3rd installment 20th–30th December.
- Spring semester (last installment 20th May): 1st installment from the date of admission to 27th February; 2nd installment 20th March–30th April; 3rd installment 20th–30th May.
- Summer semester (last installment 30th August): 1st installment from the date of admission to 2nd July; 2nd installment 20th–30th July; 3rd installment 20th–30th August.

## Which bank to pay into
- Bachelor students only: United Bank for Africa (UBA) or National Financial Credit (NFC), account name "THE ICT UNIVERSITY SCOLARITE".
- Masters and PhD students only: Société Générale Cameroun or Ecobank, account name "THE ICT UNIVERSITY SCOLARITE".
- International payments: Bank of America (account "The ICT University") or Access Bank Nigeria (account "Information & Communication Technology Global Training", dollar and naira accounts).
- Bus fee only: CCA Bank Cameroon, account name "THE ICT UNIVERSITY SCOLARITE".
- For the account numbers, IBAN and SWIFT codes, use the official Tuition Fee Payment Plan PDF or ask the finance office. Always double-check the account number before paying.
